Turtle Dash — How to Play

🐢 Turtle Controls:
Use ↑ ↓ ← → arrow keys to move the turtle

🦈 Shark Controls:
Use W S A D keys to move the shark

Turtle's Objective:
🐚 Collect shells to score points
⚠️ Don't let the shark catch you!

Shark's Objective:
Catch the turtle before they get all the shells!

👤 Solo: you're the turtle — arrows/WASD or touch to swim, Shark-Bot hunts you!
